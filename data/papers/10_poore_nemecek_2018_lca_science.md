# Reducing Food's Environmental Impacts Through Producers and Consumers

- **Citation:** Poore, J., & Nemecek, T. (2018). Science, 360(6392), 987–992.
- **DOI:** `10.1126/science.aaq0216`
- **URL:** https://ourworldindata.org/environmental-impacts-of-food

## Summary
Global meta-analysis across ~38,700 farms and 1,600 processors spanning 40 agricultural products covering cradle-to-retail greenhouse gas emissions (kg CO2e/kg), land use, acidification, eutrophication, and freshwater withdrawals.

## Key Benchmarks Used in Sampurna AI (kg CO2e / kg product, OWID Global Averages)
- Beef (beef herd): 99.48 | Fish (farmed): 13.63 | Poultry meat: 9.87 | Eggs: 4.67 | Rice: 4.45 | Wheat & Rye: 1.57 | Groundnuts: 1.23 | Peas: 0.98 | Other Pulses: 0.84.
- Demonstrates that shifting protein intake from ruminant/poultry animal systems toward optimized legume-cereal-fungal blends reduces GHG emissions by 75–98% per 20 g of complete protein.
