# Nutri Veggie Index: An Innovative Nutrition Classification Scheme for Quality Assessment of Plant-Based Meat Analogues

- **Citation:** Foods (2026)
- **PMCID:** PMC13664401
- **DOI:** `10.3390/foods15193416`
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13664401/

## Abstract
Growing availability and consumption of plant-based meat analogues (PBMAs) have highlighted the need for nutritional assessment tools specifically designed to address the unique characteristics of these products. The Nutritional Quality Index for Plant-Based Meat Analogues (NCS-Nutri Veggie Index) was developed based on expert consensus recommendations and existing nutritional assessment frameworks. The index evaluates seven domains: front-of-package nutrition labeling, characteristics associated with protein quality and quantity, lipid composition, sodium content, dietary fiber, micronutrient fortification, and the inclusion of sociobiodiversity ingredients, generating scores ranging from 0 to 100. Across 334 evaluated commercial PBMAs, most foods scored intermediate (C, 53.0%) followed by good quality (B, 33.5%).
