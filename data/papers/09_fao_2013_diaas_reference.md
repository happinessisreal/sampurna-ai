# Dietary Protein Quality Evaluation in Human Nutrition (FAO 2013 DIAAS Standard)

- **Citation:** FAO Food and Nutrition Paper 92 (2013), Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/ag/humannutrition/35978-02317b979a686a57aa4593304ffc17f06.pdf

## Summary
The 2013 FAO Expert Consultation recommended replacing PDCAAS (Protein Digestibility-Corrected Amino Acid Score) with **DIAAS** (Digestible Indispensable Amino Acid Score), which evaluates indispensable amino acid digestibility at the terminal ileum rather than over total fecal crude protein, and removes arbitrary truncation at 1.00 for individual ingredients when evaluating complementarity in mixed meals.

## Reference Pattern (Older Children, Adolescents, Adults — mg/g protein)
- Histidine: 16.0 | Isoleucine: 30.0 | Leucine: 61.0 | Lysine: 48.0 | SAA (Met+Cys): 23.0 | AAA (Phe+Tyr): 41.0 | Threonine: 25.0 | Tryptophan: 6.6 | Valine: 40.0.
- Used as Sampurna AI's primary scoring pattern alongside WHO/FAO/UNU (2007) PDCAAS for backward comparability.
